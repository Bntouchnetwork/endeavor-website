Welcome to the Partner Program!

  1. Open the email we just sent and create your password (it also turns on two-step verification).
  2. In the portal, go to "Stripe payouts" and create your Stripe account. Have your ID, your bank details and your tax number (SSN, ITIN or EIN if you are in the U.S.) at hand. You enter them directly with Stripe.
