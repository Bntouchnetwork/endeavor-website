Plan my U.S. branch

Points to evaluate

  • Taxes in both countries: Brazil and the United States do not have an income tax treaty, and Brazil has rules on the profits of foreign subsidiaries. Plan with CPAs in both countries.
  • Sending capital: the investment is sent through a regular foreign exchange contract, and assets abroad may need to be reported to the Brazilian Central Bank.
  • Visas: opening a company does not grant a visa. Immigration matters are referred to licensed immigration attorneys.

U.S. Companies in Paraguay

Produce in Paraguay. Export to the United States.

For U.S. companies that import from China today, Paraguay offers a Western Hemisphere alternative: a manufacturing base with a 1% maquila tax, low-cost clean energy and lower average U.S. tariff exposure. Endeavor helps you evaluate, structure and coordinate the operation.

  • ~11% vs. ~24% from China

    Lower U.S. tariff exposure

    In August 2026, the average effective U.S. tariff was about 11% on goods from Paraguay and about 24% on goods from China.¹

  • 1% single maquila tax

    Maquila tax regime

    A single 1% tax on the value added in Paraguay or on the export invoice, with exemption from VAT, corporate income tax and dividend tax.²

  • 0% duties on temporary imports

    Duty-free inputs and machinery

    Raw materials, machinery and supplies can enter temporarily without import duties when they are processed for export.²

  • $0.03-0.05 per kWh, industrial

    Low-cost clean energy

    Electricity comes from the Itaipú and Yacyretá hydroelectric dams, providing renewable power at some of the lowest industrial rates in the region.³

  • 29 median age

    Young, competitive workforce

    A young, growing labor force, with average monthly manufacturing wages of roughly $350-550.³

  • 1-2 h from U.S. Eastern Time

    Same hemisphere, same workday

    Real-time collaboration with your U.S. team instead of a 12-hour time difference, with easier visits, audits and quality control.

  • Diversify your supply chain

    Reduced concentration risk

    A second production base outside China lowers exposure to country-specific tariffs, trade actions and long-distance disruptions.

  • 0.5% free trade zone income tax

    Free trade zones and Mercosur

    Free trade zones offer a special 0.5% income tax, and Paraguay's Mercosur membership gives preferential access to Brazil and Argentina.²

How Endeavor helps

  • Feasibility review: Paraguay production vs. importing from China
  • U.S. and Paraguayan entity structure
  • Maquila registration coordinated with licensed Paraguayan partners
  • Banking, financing and cross-border compliance coordination
Evaluate a Paraguay operation

Points to evaluate

  • Country of origin: U.S. tariff treatment depends on origin rules; simply assembling imported parts may not make a product "Made in Paraguay."
  • Logistics: Paraguay is landlocked; exports move by river barge or truck to Atlantic ports.
  • Rates change: U.S. tariffs change frequently and vary by product. Confirm current rates for your HS codes.

¹ Penn Wharton Budget Model (China) and USAFacts (Paraguay), August 2026 data; averages vary by product mix. ² PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Paraguay; Maquila Law No. 7.547/2025 and Free Trade Zone Law No. 523/95. ³ LatAm FDI, industrial parks in Paraguay. Figures are indicative and should be confirmed for each project.
